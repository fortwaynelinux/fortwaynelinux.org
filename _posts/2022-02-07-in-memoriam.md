Benjamin A. Landin
1989-2022

It is with great sadness that we report the passing of our friend Ben, who died Monday afternoon at the the age of 33 after a month-long illness. He was a principal maintainer of the Fort Wayne Linux Users Group website for the past five years. Ben also usually took the initiative for scheduling events and reserving facilities for meetings, and lent his equipment, including his projector and portable desktop, so that others could make the most effective presentations possible. He suggested, and was instrumental in effecting, the switch from [Meetup](https://meetup.com) to [GetTogether](https://gettogether.community) for event scheduling.

Ben attended [Homestead High School](https://homestead.sacs.k12.in.us/), [The Career Academy High School at Anthis](https://fortwayneschools.org/careeracademy), and [Ivy Tech Community College - Northeast](https://www.ivytech.edu/fortwayne/index.html) in Fort Wayne. He was most recently employed by [Silicon Mountain Technologies](https://www.siliconmtn.com/) as a systems administrator. Ben is survived by his parents, brother, and his many other relatives and friends.

Ben enjoyed online gaming, especially [Minecraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minecraft), and maintained a Minecraft server. He was also a [Dungeons & Dragons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeons_&_Dragons) aficionado, and a regular at former [Game Annex](https://www.facebook.com/thegameannex/) in Fort Wayne. 

Ben was a staunch supporter of the [Electronic Frontier Foundation](https://www.eff.org/about), so much so that he usually overrode the objections over other leadership team members who thought we were sharing too many EFF notices and calls-to-action. It is suggested that memorials be made to the EFF by [donating here](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/join-eff-4).

Ben deliberately designed most systems so that others could step up and maintain the site if anything ever happened to him, something we jokingly used to refer to as the "hit by a bus principle." The group has redundant domain names, so even if one of them registered in his name can't be recovered, the leadership team will still have access to the other. There is also shared access to the Fort Wayne LUG repository.

One exigency that was not foreseen was the loss of Ben's participation in accessing the group's [multi-signature Bitcoin wallet](https://en.bitcoin.it/wiki/Multi-signature). Accordingly, we have removed the Bitcoin donation address from the website until we can determine if restoring access is possible.

A link to the official obituary will be posted here as soon as it is available. On behalf of the entire Fort Wayne Linux Users Group, we offer our deepest condolences to all who knew and loved Ben.
